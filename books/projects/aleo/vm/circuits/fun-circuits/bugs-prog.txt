Program-crate circuit formalization notes
=========================================

16 rows are fully flagged 11111. ProgramID::to_address is 10001.
OutputID::record is 10101 (honest-witness completeness only).
135 catalog rows stay at 0.
Flags are 1 only where a real define/defruled is present and the
statement is the one named by the column. No skip-proofs were used.
ACL2 was not run.

Spec gaps (snarkVM circuit vs funspec)
--------------------------------------

1. Plaintext bit cache (confirmed).

   Plaintext::from_bits_le_internal / from_bits_be_internal
   (circuit/program/src/data/plaintext/from_bits.rs) store the entire
   input bit vector in the OnceCell that sits beside the parsed value:

       cell.set(bits_le.to_vec())   // likewise bits_be

   That vector is the raw input, including any bits the parser did not
   consume. write_bits_le / write_bits_be (to_bits.rs) return
   bits.get_or_init(...), so once the cell is full they do not
   recompute the canonical encoding.

   The funspecs plaintext-to-bits-le / plaintext-to-bits-be recompute
   bits from the structured plaintext (values-plaintext-to-bits).
   plaintext-to-fields packs those recomputed bits. plaintext-size-in-fields
   and plaintext-num-randomizers take the length of the recomputed bits.

   Consequences for a plaintext built by from_bits:

   - Soundness fails. The circuit does not constrain the cached bits to
     equal the canonical encoding. A satisfying assignment of the parser
     can return, from write_bits_*, the raw input variables.
   - Completeness fails when the input is not exactly the canonical
     encoding. Trailing bits are ignored by the parser and by the funspec,
     but they stay in the cache and are what write_bits_* / to_fields emit.
   - Nested values cache their own slices, and the outer value caches the
     whole input. Outer write_bits returns that outer cache rather than
     walking the children.

   Affected rows: Plaintext::from_bits_le, from_bits_be,
   from_bits_le_internal, from_bits_be_internal, write_bits_le,
   write_bits_be, to_fields, size_in_fields, num_randomizers,
   and encrypt / encrypt_symmetric / encrypt_with_randomizers, which
   add randomizers to to_fields. to_bits_raw does not read this cache.

2. OutputID::record accepts a zero sender ciphertext (confirmed).

   circuit/program/src/response/mod.rs OutputID::record assert_eq's the
   commitment and the checksum, then asserts

       is_equal(sender, expected) OR is_zero(sender)

   crypto-make-output-id-record returns the output id of the expected
   ciphertext unconditionally. The circuit also accepts a public sender
   ciphertext of 0 when the expected ciphertext is nonzero, and stores
   that 0. Soundness against the funspec does not hold for that branch,
   and there is no PFCS equivalence.
   The honest snarkVM witness sets the public ciphertext equal to the
   expected one (the multiplier is 1 when that value is 0, because the
   inverse does not exist). crypto-make-output-id-record-gadget-complete
   is that witness, including the zero-comparison arithmetic, and
   COMPLETE is 1 only for it. SOUND stays 0.
   R1CS-SPEC is 1: the gadget is the two assert_eq constraints, the two
   field inequalities, the product that is the OR via De Morgan, and
   assert of the OR bit.

Checked, not a spec gap
-----------------------

Square-root ordering.
  Field::square_roots_flagged_nondeterministic does not constrain which
  root is first (circuit/types/field/src/square_root.rs). Program casts
  that recover a group from an x-coordinate
  (Field::cast to Address or Group, via Group::from_x_coordinate /
  from_x_coordinate_flagged) still determine a unique point: at most one
  of (x, y) and (x, -y) lies in the prime-order subgroup, and the circuit
  enforces subgroup membership. The lesser-root choice is only the
  witness. values-group-from-x / field-cast agree with that unique point.
  No program-crate row is flagged down for square-root ordering.

Cast ranges.
  Field::cast to Boolean asserts is_zero OR is_one; field-cast returns
  an error otherwise. Integer::from_field and Scalar::from_field fail
  when the field is outside 2^width or the scalar modulus;
  values-int-from-field and values-scalar-from-field do the same.
  cast_lossy truncates (low bits, LSB, or Elligator2) on both sides.
  Boolean::cast to IdentifierLiteral fails on both sides (0x00 and 0x01
  are not identifier bytes). No cast row asserts a range the funspec
  omits, or omits a range the funspec asserts.

Zero-test multiplier.
  Field::is_not_equal's witness uses multiplier 1 when the difference
  has no inverse (equal.rs). Field::is_zero is is_equal to zero, so the
  multiplier is 1 when the field is 0. field-is-zero-gadget-complete
  uses multiplier 0 in that case. Both assignments satisfy
  (x)*(m)=(r) and (x)*(1-r)=(0). The program-crate completeness
  theorems use snarkVM's multiplier 1. This is not a spec gap.

Hash abstraction (not a snarkVM bug)
------------------------------------

Poseidon, BHP, Pedersen, and Keccak are not expanded. Where the only
non-hash step is wrapping the hash output (ProgramID::to_address, which
is hash_to_group_psd4 then Address::from_group), the gadget is empty.
Soundness is not claimed: an empty gadget does not determine the address,
and assuming the address variable already equals program-id-to-address
does not prove it.  Flags are 10001 (gadget and completeness of the empty
constraint list only).

Other hash users also emit to_bits, equality, or encryption constraints.
Those constraints are not fully listed, so those rows stay partial.
Examples: compute_function_id, record serial_number / to_commitment /
encrypt / decrypt, request verify / check_input_ids / to_tpk,
response from_outputs / process_outputs_from_callback,
dynamic-record merkleize_data, state-path verify.

Unproved rows
-------------

OutputID::record.
  See spec gap 2. Sample constraints hold over the prime 5 for the
  honest witness (commitment 4, checksum 2, ciphertext 3). Completeness
  of that witness is proved. Soundness and PFCS equivalence are absent
  because of the zero branch.

Field::cast to Boolean is proved as field-cast-to-boolean-gadget
(is_zero, is_one, De Morgan OR, assert; sample x = 1 over the prime 5).
The catalog row also casts to address, group, integer, scalar, and
identifier. Those callees are not proved, and field-cast-gadget stays
nil, so the row stays 0.

Integer::cast and Scalar::cast to Boolean are the same zero-or-one
assert. Integer is_zero / is_one are proved in int-equal.lisp, but each
catalog row also casts through to_bits or from_x_coordinate. Those
callees are not proved here, so the rows stay 0.

Future, Entry, Argument, Literal, Value, and Plaintext equality walk a
variable-length collection (or, for Literal, a sum of group, integer,
and string equality). They are not a fixed handful of field equalities.
DynamicRecord equality is the AND of two group equalities, one field
equality, and one integer equality (17 constraints in the
variable/variable tests). That chain is not discharged. Ciphertext
equality is the same variable-length field walk.

Every other partial row has a define of a gadget whose body is nil and
whose :short says the real constraints are not expanded, so R1CS-SPEC
is 0. Where the funspec is definitionally the callee, or is boolean
negation of the sibling equality, a defruled NAME-funspecs-match is in
prog-catalog.lisp. That lemma is not an R1CS soundness theorem.

Those match lemmas are:

  plaintext-from, plaintext-from-bits-le, plaintext-from-bits-be,
  plaintext-num-randomizers, identifier-from-bits-be,
  identifier-from-field, boolean-cast, dynamic-record-is-not-equal,
  crypto-record-is-not-equal, crypto-ciphertext-is-not-equal,
  crypto-record-encrypt, future-is-not-equal, argument-is-not-equal,
  plaintext-is-not-equal, value-is-not-equal, literal-is-not-equal,
  entry-is-not-equal.

Why the remaining gadgets are not expanded
------------------------------------------

Field/integer/scalar/group bit decomposition (to_bits, from_bits,
from_field, casts through to_field) is the booleanity constraint, the
weighted-sum assert_eq, and the less-than-modulus check. Those are not
reproved here; rows that are only that decomposition, or that forward
to it (write_bits_* of identifier, program id, literals, leaves,
records, futures, values, ciphertexts), stay partial.

Group scalar multiplication and Edwards addition (Request::to_tpk,
record serial numbers that multiply by the cofactor, address casts that
call from_x_coordinate) are not expanded to the curve formulas.

Variable-length structural recursion (plaintext, future, record, and
value equality, find, to_bits, to_fields, encrypt-with-randomizers) is
a data-dependent chain of the field and boolean gadgets above. The
fixed-length cases that were discharged are Identifier, ProgramID, and
DynamicFuture equality, and Record::is_hiding. DynamicRecord equality is the AND of
two group equalities (each an AND of x and y field equalities), one
field equality, and one integer equality: 17 constraints in the
variable/variable tests. That chain is not discharged.
