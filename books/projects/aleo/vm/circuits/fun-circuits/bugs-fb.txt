Field::div_unchecked, both operands variable, x = y = 0.
The single constraint (q) * (y) = (x) holds for every quotient q.
field-div-unchecked-funspec returns 0, which is the witness snarkVM
computes, but a satisfying assignment may use any other q.  Soundness
is stated only for y ≠ 0.  Completeness holds for the witness q = 0,
and for the unique quotient when y ≠ 0.  y = 0 and x ≠ 0 is unsatisfiable,
matching the funspec error.

Field::to_non_unique_bits_le.
The gadget is boolean checks plus assert_eq of the field with the
weighted sum of the bits.  There is no range check.  The canonical
253 bits satisfy it (completeness).  Any other bit vector with the
same residue modulo (base-prime) also satisfies it, so a satisfying
assignment need not equal field-to-non-unique-bits-le-funspec.
R1CS-SOUND is 0.

Field::to_lower_bits_le and to_lower_bits_be, k = 253.
Same shape: boolean checks and assert_eq with k bits.  For k <= 252,
2^k < (base-prime), the bits are unique, and soundness is proved under
that hypothesis.  For k = 253 the residue is not unique (for example the
bits of 0 and the bits of the prime are distinct and both sum to 0 in
the field), so a satisfying assignment can differ from the canonical
bits returned by the funspec.  Completeness of the canonical witness
still holds for k <= 253.

Field::square_roots_flagged_nondeterministic.
The square constraint does not order the two roots.  The assignment
(greater root, its negation, flag 0) satisfies the gadget whenever x is
a nonzero square, while field-square-roots-flagged-nondeterministic-funspec
returns the lesser root first.  The Rust source documents this.  R1CS-SOUND
and R1CS-COMPLETE are 0: the ordered witness was not proved.

Not bugs; the proofs were not found in the enable-only style:

Field comparisons (is_less_than, is_greater_than, is_greater_than_or_equal,
is_less_than_or_equal).  Soundness to the funspec is proved.
Completeness needs the field-to-bits and bitwise-comparison witnesses.
No PFCS.  No defrule sample (the witness list for (base-prime) is 253 bits,
and a hand-built p=5 witness for the whole less-than gadget was not checked).

Field::pow.  Soundness reuses field-pow-soundness.  A sample at prime 5
is checked.  Completeness (every square, product, and range witness) and
a PFCS of the 253-bit loop were not proved.

Field::square_root.  field-square-root-soundness gives y*y = x and
y <= (p-1)/2.  Equality with field-square-root-funspec needs uniqueness
of that lesser root and was not proved.  No sample, no PFCS, no completeness.

Field::even_square_root.  The gadget and a prime-5 sample are written.
Equality with field-even-square-root-funspec was not proved.  No completeness, no PFCS.

Field::from_bits_le and from_bits_be.  The gadget matches snarkVM: the sum
is a linear combination, excess bits contribute one sum-to-zero constraint,
and a full-width list contributes bits-lte-const-check.  Soundness is
proved only for lists shorter than the field.  The full-width case needs
the range-check witness and was not connected to the funspec.  No PFCS, no completeness.

Field::write_bits_le and write_bits_be.  Soundness reuses
field-to-bits-soundness.  Completeness of the range-check witnesses and a
PFCS were not proved.  Samples at prime 5 are checked.

Field::to_upper_bits_be and to_upper_bits_le.  The gadget and a modular
characterization are written, with a prime-5 sample.  The funspec
(nthcdr of the canonical bits, and the lower-bits-are-zero error) was
not proved.  No PFCS, no completeness.

Field::is_less_than_or_equal_constant and assert_less_than_or_equal_constant.
Soundness reuses bits-lte-const / bits-lte-const-check.  PFCS and samples
are for the constant bits (0 1) only; the general constraint shape depends
on the constant.  Completeness for an arbitrary constant was not proved.
