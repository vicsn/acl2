Curve, scalar, address, and account circuit formalization gaps.
A flag in circspec-crv.tsv is 1 only when a defruled or defthm (no skip-proofs)
discharges that obligation.  Theorems that stop at snarkVM's local equations,
or that assume a component result instead of deriving it, are not counted.

1. Subgroup membership is not a consequence of the arithmetic constraints.
   group-new-funspec, group-from-xy-coordinates-funspec,
   group-from-x-coordinate-funspec, group-enforce-in-group-funspec,
   address-new-funspec, address-from-field-funspec, address-from-funspec,
   address-from-bits-le-funspec, address-from-bits-be-funspec,
   compute-key-new-funspec, and signature-new-funspec all require the point
   to be in the prime-order subgroup (groupp).  snarkVM's enforce_in_group
   posts a witness Q, enforces the curve equation on Q, and checks P = 4Q
   by two doublings.  That is the cofactor argument (the image of [4] is the
   subgroup of order r on a curve of order 4r).  The R1CS does not contain
   a proof of that group-order fact, and the doublings are the dedicated
   formulas below, which are not yet identified with ecurve::edwards-bls12-add.
   Off-curve inputs make curve-raw-double / curve-raw-add return spec-error
   when a denominator is zero; the funspecs that quantify only over curve
   points do not record that halt.  R1CS-SOUND and R1CS-COMPLETE are 0 for
   these functions.  from_x_coordinate_flagged additionally calls
   square_roots_flagged_nondeterministic and is_in_group on both candidate
   points; those circuits are not expanded here.

2. Group addition, subtraction, doubling, enforce_double, and mul_by_cofactor.
   The variable/variable gadgets are the constraints snarkVM emits:
   add is the four products (u, v0, v1, v0*v1) and the two division enforces,
   with a = -1 inlined so U = (x1+y1)*(x2+y2) and a*v0 = -v0;
   sub is that system with x2 negated (negation is a coefficient);
   double / enforce_double are xy, x^2, y^2 and the two doubling enforces;
   mul_by_cofactor is two doublings.
   crv-group-add-gadget-to-products and
   crv-group-enforce-on-curve-gadget-to-equation rewrite those constraints
   to the field products.  They do not prove equality with
   ecurve::edwards-bls12-add (group-add-funspec, group-sub-funspec,
   group-double-funspec, group-mul-by-cofactor-funspec) or with
   point-on-twisted-edwards-p (group-enforce-on-curve-funspec).
   The missing steps are the y-numerator rearrangement
   U + a*v0 - v1 = y1*y2 - a*x1*x2, the dedicated-doubling identity
   with edwards-bls12-add of a point with itself, and the rearrangement of
   y^2*(d*x^2-1) = a*x^2-1 into a*x^2 + y^2 = 1 + d*x^2*y^2.
   On this complete curve the denominators are nonzero for curve points;
   that fact is part of the guard of twisted-edwards-add and is not
   re-exported as a hypothesis-free rewrite used here.
   R1CS-SOUND and R1CS-COMPLETE are 0.  add_assign and sub_assign are the
   same gadgets as add and sub.

3. Group scalar multiplication (Group::mul, Group::mul_assign, and every
   caller of A::g_scalar_multiply).
   mul_assign walks the 251 big-endian scalar bits.  For each bit it doubles
   the accumulator and always adds the base (even when the bit is 0), then
   selects with a field ternary on each coordinate.  The first iteration
   constant-folds double of the zero point and addition of the zero point.
   crv-group-mul-step-gadget is one variable step.  There is no theorem that
   the 251-fold iteration equals ecurve::edwards-bls12-mul, nor that the
   scalar to_bits_be witness is the canonical 251 bits.  Consequently
   compute-key-from-private-key, private-key-to-compute-key,
   compute-key-to-address, view-key-to-address, and signature-verify are
   not linked to their funspecs.  Hash steps in those functions are abstract:
   a theorem may assume the hash output equals the funspec hash, but the
   group operations around the hash are not discharged.
   ComputeKey::from has no non-hash constraint; its theorems assume
   sk_prf = aleo-hash-to-scalar-psd4-funspec and are counted.
   signature-verify's final check is boolean and of challenge is_equal and
   address is_equal.  Those equality gadgets are formalized, but the
   candidate challenge (HashToScalar_psd8) and the candidate address
   (to_address) are not.

4. Address comparisons.
   is_less_than is field-lt-gadget on the x-coordinates.
   is_greater_than swaps the addresses.
   is_greater_than_or_equal and is_less_than_or_equal are the same less-than
   constraints with result linear combination 1 - r.
   Soundness is crv-address-is-*-gadget-sound, from field-lt-gadget-soundness.
   field-lt-gadget has no completeness theorem, so R1CS-COMPLETE is 0.
   The 253-bit unrolling is not a pfcs::parse-def, so PFCS-SPEC is 0.
   No concrete satisfying assignment is given, so R1CS-SAMPLES is 0.

5. Group and address write_bits.
   The constraints are field-to-bits-gadget on the x-coordinate.
   Soundness (crv-group-write-bits-le-gadget-sound and the address and
   big-endian variants) uses field-to-bits-soundness and
   acl2::nat=>lebits-of-lebits=>nat.  Big-endian is the reversal; it adds
   no constraint.  field-to-bits-gadget has no completeness theorem, and the
   bit-length check is not a lifted PFCS definition.  R1CS-COMPLETE,
   PFCS-SPEC, and R1CS-SAMPLES are 0.
   ComputeKey::write_bits is the concatenation of the two group writings
   (pk_sig then pr_sig); that concatenation is not a separate theorem.
   Scalar::write_bits_le is to_lower_bits_le of 251 bits (boolean checks and
   one power-of-two assert_eq, which forces the value below 2^251) plus
   Boolean::assert_less_than_or_equal_constant against the bits of r-1.
   That comparator is a fold of boolean and/or against the constant bits,
   then an assert.  It is not formalized.  Signature::write_bits is the
   scalar writings of challenge and response followed by the compute-key
   writing.

6. Scalar comparison.
   crv-scalar-is-less-than-gadget is the field decomposition of the linear
   combination 2*(a-b): boolean checks, the power-of-two constraint whose
   left side is that combination, and the range check.  The result bit is
   the first little-endian bit.  is_greater_than swaps a and b.
   is_greater_than_or_equal uses the less-than gadget and reads 1 - bit;
   is_less_than_or_equal swaps and then negates.  For scalars, 2*(a-b) mod p
   is odd iff a < b, because 2r < p: the representative is 2*(a-b) when
   a >= b and p + 2*(a-b) when a < b.  That parity lemma is not proved, so
   R1CS-SOUND and R1CS-COMPLETE are 0.  The decomposition is not a lifted
   PFCS definition, and there is no sample assignment.

7. Scalar addition and casts.
   AddAssign converts both scalars to the base field (no constraint), adds
   them (linear combination; 2r < p so the sum does not wrap the base field),
   takes to_lower_bits_le of 252 bits, rebuilds that value (linear
   combination), compares it with the scalar modulus, selects with a ternary,
   then takes to_lower_bits_le of 251 bits and range-checks against r-1.
   Scalar::add is that AddAssign.  from_bits_le is the same range check
   (and assert_bits_are_zero on bits past 251).  from_bits_be reverses then
   calls from_bits_le.  from_field is from_bits_le of the field's 253 bits.
   from_field_lossy is from_bits_le of the low 250 bits.  None of these
   range checks or lower-bit decompositions are proved against the funspecs.

8. ComputeKey::is_equal / is_not_equal and Signature::is_equal / is_not_equal.
   The gadgets are defined (two group equalities, one scalar equality, and
   the boolean ands; signature adds two scalar equalities around the compute
   key).  crv-compute-key-is-equal-gadget-sound assumes the three component
   result bits instead of deriving them from the appended constraints, so
   R1CS-SOUND is 0.  The disequality theorems only rewrite 1-out once out is
   assumed equal to the equality funspec.  The compute-key PFCS definition
   is lifted but not proved equivalent to the funspec.  A sample assignment
   of two equal zero-ish keys is given for the compute-key gadgets.

9. Signature::ternary.
   The gadget is seven field ternaries (challenge, response, and the five
   compute-key coordinates).  The PFCS predicate is proved sound and
   complete against signature-ternary-funspec.  There is no separate R1CS
   defruled that opens the appended gadget, so R1CS-SOUND and R1CS-COMPLETE
   are 0.  The sample checks the constraints for condition 0, which selects
   the second signature.

10. Empty constraint systems.
    Address-to-field (Field::from an address), ComputeKey::to_fields,
    and Signature::to_fields add no constraint.  Their theorems identify
    the coordinate projections with the funspecs.
    ComputeKey::from also adds no non-hash constraint, but sk_prf is the
    Poseidon hash.  crv-compute-key-from-under-hash-assumption assumes that
    hash output, so R1CS-SOUND and PFCS-SPEC are 0.  Completeness of the
    empty constraint list is kept.
