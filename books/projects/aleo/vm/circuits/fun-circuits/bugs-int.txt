Integer circuit formalization gaps and bug check
================================================

Compared snarkVM circuit/types/integers (under /tmp/snarkvm-src) with
books/projects/aleo/vm/circuits/fun-specs/integers.lisp.

No soundness bug (constraints accept a wrong output) and no completeness
bug (a funspec value cannot be witnessed) was established.  The gaps below
are unfinished proofs or circuits that the existing r1cs/ books do not
cover for every width.  ACL2 admitted the integer books that these notes
name.  A row's R1CS-SOUND or R1CS-COMPLETE flag stays 0 when any width or
signedness that the row claims is still unproved.

1. mul_checked, mul, mul_assign, mul_and_check
   snarkVM uses one field product of the bit-weighted sums when
   2 * BITS < 252, i.e. widths 8, 16, 32 and 64, unsigned or as the
   inner step of signed mul after abs_wrapped.  Width 128 uses
   karatsuba_multiply and then requires the high half of z1 and z2 to be
   zero.  int-mul-checked-unsigned-small-sound and
   int-mul-and-check-pred-to-funspec assume (not (equal n 128)) and
   (not signedp).  The r1cs book unsigned-small-mul-and-check-gadget-soundness
   is inside a #|| WIP ||# comment, so it is not used.
   Signed mul_checked (absolute values, positive-overflow and
   negative-underflow asserts, then ternary on the sign) is not proved.

2. mul_wrapped
   The Babbage circuit is unsigned-small-mul-wrapped (signed-small-mul-wrapped
   is the same constraints).  Its soundness needs the n + n/2 + 1 bit
   window to fit in the base field, which fails for 128.  The theorem
   int-mul-wrapped-small-sound is unsigned and excludes 128.  There is no
   sample of the lowlow/highlow/lowhigh witnesses.

3. mul_with_flags
   For 2 * BITS < 252 the flag is field not-equal of the witnessed product
   and the field product.  For 128 bits the flag is not-equal of
   (z1-upper + z2) and zero.  Because those values are below the field
   prime, that is zero iff both are zero, which matches
   int-mul-with-flags-spec.  No defrule connects the circuit to the spec,
   and the PFCS leaves the computed product as a free variable.  All five
   flags are 0.

4. karatsuba_multiply
   The Rust decomposition z0 = x0*y0, z2 = x1*y1, z1 = (x1+x0)*(y1+y0)-z2-z0
   matches int-karatsuba-multiply-spec.  Only the z0 constraint is defined
   and sampled.  The upper-bit split and z2 are not proved.

5. unsigned_division_via_witness, div, div_assign, div_checked, div_wrapped,
   rem, rem_assign, rem_checked, rem_wrapped, modulo
   r1cs/unsigned-small-div proves the Euclidean quotient and remainder,
   including remainder < divisor, when 2*n+1 < integer-length(p).  That
   holds for 8..64 and fails for 128 (int-width-div-fits).
   int-unsigned-division-small-sound is that connection for the unsigned
   gadget.  snarkVM's 128-bit branch is
   quotient.mul_checked(divisor).add_checked(remainder), i.e.
   unsigned-medium-div, and is not connected.
   Signed div/rem take absolute values, divide, and restore the sign.
   That composition is not proved, so R1CS-SOUND stays 0 on these rows.
   int-div-pred-to-euclid is only the field equation x = q*y + r.  The
   remainder < divisor constraint is in the R1CS gadget and is not in that
   PFCS.
   modulo on a signed integer is a host halt.  int-modulo-signed-is-error
   matches the funspec.  Unsigned modulo is rem_wrapped.
   int-unsigned-division-sample-holds is a one-bit witness of
   unsigned-small-div-gadget, so R1CS-SAMPLES is 1.  int-div-sample-holds
   is only the Euclidean field equation.

6. is_less_than and the other Compare methods
   Unsigned less-than is unsigned-small-lt.  z = 0 iff x < y, and the
   boolean snarkVM returns is the linear combination 1 - z.  The
   hypothesis n+1 < integer-length(p) holds for 128 as well
   (int-width-add-fits).  int-is-less-than-unsigned-gadget-sound and
   int-is-less-than-unsigned-gadget-complete state this for every integer
   width.  The completeness witness is the low n bits of 2^n + x - y.
   The unsigned swaps and negations (is_greater_than,
   is_greater_than_or_equal, is_less_than_or_equal) are the same gadget
   and are sound and complete for the unsigned case.  Their samples hold.
   Signed less-than in compare.rs is not the library gadget.
   int-is-less-than-signed-gadget is the high bit of 2^n + N(x) - N(y),
   xor of the sign bits, msb(x) AND NOT msb(y), and a ternary whose
   condition is the linear combination 1 - diff.  N is the unsigned
   reading of the bit pattern.  int-is-less-than-signed-gadget-sound
   and int-is-less-than-signed-gadget-complete prove that circuit for
   every integer width, including 128.  The completeness witness is
   those low bits, the sign bits, and the ternary selector; the output
   wire is proved equal to the funspec and is not assumed to be it.
   Greater-than swaps the operands.  The or-equal forms negate the
   opposite less-than bit and add no constraint.  The same four
   theorems, with the arguments swapped or the result bit negated,
   cover is_greater_than, is_greater_than_or_equal, and
   is_less_than_or_equal.  R1CS-SOUND and R1CS-COMPLETE are 1.
   No snarkVM soundness or completeness bug was found in compare.rs.

7. from_field_lossy
   This is Field::to_bits_le, then the first n bits.  Soundness is
   field-to-bits-soundness plus lebits=>nat of the low bits = f mod 2^n.
   It is not restated as int-from-field-lossy-spec, and there is no
   completeness theorem: bits-lt-prime's witness is not constructed.
   No sample of the 253-bit gadget.

8. from_bits_le, from_bits_be
   Exactly n bits (the path Integer::new uses) adds no constraint; the
   value is int-wrap of the little-endian number.  That case is
   int-from-bits-le-gadget-empty-sound.  Extra bits are one
   assert_bits_are_zero constraint (sum of the excess bits is 0).
   int-excess-bits-sum-forces-all-zero says a satisfying sum of bits
   shorter than the prime is the all-zero list.  int-from-bits-le-gadget-sound
   and int-from-bits-le-gadget-complete are that fact for every width and
   both signednesses, so R1CS-SOUND and R1CS-COMPLETE are 1.
   from_bits_be reverses, then calls from_bits_le.  Reversal is not a
   constraint.  int-from-bits-be-gadget-sound and
   int-from-bits-be-gadget-complete use int-from-bits-be-spec-is-le-of-rev.

9. Signed checked addition and subtraction, completeness
   Soundness of add_checked and sub_checked uses the library gadgets and
   holds for every width, including 128, because n+1 < 253.
   Signed completeness is int-add-checked-signed-gadget-complete and
   int-sub-checked-signed-gadget-complete: the overflow bit the circuit
   forces to 0 is 0 whenever the funspec is not spec-error.  That holds
   for every width, including 128, so R1CS-COMPLETE is 1 on add, sub,
   and their assign/checked rows.
   add_wrapped is complete for both signednesses and every width, including
   128.  int-add-wrapped-unsigned-gadget-complete and
   int-add-wrapped-signed-gadget-complete assign the wrapped result bits
   and the canonical carry int-add-carry.  int-add-wrapped-carry-equation
   forces that carry from the funspec.  The library witness is the
   unsigned-small-add gadget on those n+1 bits.
   sub_wrapped is sound only for unsigned, so its soundness flag stays 0.

10. neg, abs_checked, abs_wrapped
    Unsigned neg halts.  int-neg-unsigned-spec-error / int-neg-unsigned-complete
    say the funspec is spec-error, so there is no valid output to witness.
    Signed neg is signed-small-neg (wrapped subtraction from zero, then
    both sign bits cannot be 1).  Soundness is int-neg-signed-gadget-sound
    for every width.  Completeness is int-neg-signed-gadget-complete:
    the result bits are the bits of the negation, the carry is
    int-sub-carry of zero minus the operand, and int-neg-sign-and-is-zero
    forces the both-negative bit to 0 whenever the funspec is not
    spec-error.  That holds for every width, including 128, so
    R1CS-COMPLETE is 1.  Unsigned negation is not given a circuit.
    The PFCS is only the sign-and check, so PFCS-SPEC stays 0.
    Unsigned abs is the identity (empty gadget), proved sound and complete.
    Signed abs_checked completeness is
    int-abs-checked-signed-gadget-complete, and signed abs_wrapped
    completeness is int-abs-wrapped-signed-gadget-complete, every width
    including 128.  int-abs-checked-overflow-is-zero says the overflow
    bit is 0 whenever abs_checked is not spec-error.  The signed minimum
    wraps to itself under abs_wrapped.  R1CS-COMPLETE is 1 on both rows.

11. pow, pow_checked, pow_wrapped, shifts
    Variable pow_checked is square-and-multiply with mul_with_flags.
    pow_wrapped uses mul_wrapped.  Both inherit the multiplication gap,
    and the induction over the exponent bits is not proved.
    int-pow-checked-zero-exponent and int-pow-wrapped-zero-exponent show
    x^0 = 1.  The sample is one checked multiplication.
    shl / shl_assign / shl_checked check that exponent bits at and above
    log2(n) are zero (int-from-bits-le-gadget).  That check is
    int-exponent-bound-gadget-sound for every width, including 128.
    For unsigned n other than 128, int-shl-field-mul-by-pow2 is one
    constraint (x)*(2^e) = (z), and int-shl-field-mul-in-range-is-checked-spec
    matches int-shl-checked-spec when that product is already in range.
    The exponent bits are not assembled into 2^e, width 128 uses
    pow_checked, and signed shl_checked also checks sign extension.
    Those three are not proved, so R1CS-SOUND stays 0.
    int-shl-checked-errors-when-exponent-wide matches the funspec for
    e >= n; the constraint proof of that case is the exponent-bound gadget.
    shr_checked is the same exponent bound plus shr_wrapped.
    int-shr-checked-is-wrapped-when-in-range is the funspec agreement for
    e < n.  The right shift itself is not proved.
    shl_wrapped and shr_wrapped have a field-multiply sample and
    no named gadget, so R1CS-SPEC stays 0.

12. to_fields, to_scalar, is_zero, is_one
    to_fields and to_scalar are empty gadgets.  Their PFCS are
    int-to-fields-pred-to-funspec and int-to-scalar-pred-to-funspec,
    so PFCS-SPEC is 1.  to_field's empty PFCS is
    int-to-field-pred-to-funspec.
    is_zero and is_one are is_equal against 0 and 1.  The book
    int-equal.lisp certifies.  R1CS soundness and completeness for every
    integer width are int-is-not-equal-gadget-sound,
    int-is-not-equal-gadget-complete, and the equal, zero, and one
    variants.  The honest not-equal witness is z = 0 and w = 0 when the
    integers agree, and z = 1 and w the inverse of the difference of the
    unsigned readings when they differ.  int-equal-when-same-mod forces
    that witness: equal residues modulo 2^n are the same integer.
    PFCS-SPEC is 1.  int-is-zero-pred-to-funspec and
    int-is-one-pred-to-funspec are the field not-equal of the integer's
    field image against 0 and against 1.

Not constraint bugs
-------------------
shr_checked's comment says the upper exponent bits are checked nonzero.
The code calls Boolean::assert_bits_are_zero.  The comment is wrong; the
circuit matches int-shr-checked-spec.

Mul's Metrics::count delegates to DivChecked::count.  That mis-reports a
constraint count.  It does not change the constraints.

Checked operations on two constants are host evaluations (halt on
overflow) and add no constraint.  The gadgets are the variable case, which
is the case that emits R1CS.
